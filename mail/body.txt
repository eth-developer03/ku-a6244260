BUSINESS IDEAS
1. Source-grounded checker for AI-drafted clinical summaries (score 4.0; effort 4, cost 2, fit 5)
   A layer that sits on top of any AI discharge-summary or clinical-note drafter and links every generated sentence to the EMR record it came from. Unsupported statements are flagged for the clinician to review before signing. Sell it to Indian hospitals as a per-bed or per-document API. Keep marketing to verification, not accuracy claims, to avoid overpromising.
   Why now: MDCalc's CrossCover launch treats clinicians' trust in generative AI answers as a live problem, and Theranos's history shows what happens when validation claims outrun evidence.
   First step: Take 20 AI-drafted summaries from your internship workflow and mark every sentence that has no matching source in the record; use the error rate to scope the checker.

2. Reimbursement payouts for Indian SMEs via UPI and IMPS (score 4.0; effort 3, cost 3, fit 5)
   A small API and dashboard that lets Indian companies with 20 to 200 staff run employee expense and benefit reimbursements. It validates bank accounts, batches payouts, and tracks status and reconciliation. Build it on a licensed payout partner such as Cashfree or Razorpay so you avoid holding funds or needing a payment aggregator licence yourself.
   Why now: Flex's engineer #1 story is about fixing HSA and FSA payment failures from firsthand experience, showing that benefit payout reliability is a sharp, underserved pain point.
   First step: Pull the payout API docs from two licensed Indian partners and list the bank-validation and batch endpoints you would need, then message five HR or finance leads at small firms about their current reimbursement pain.

3. Report collection tracker for Indian clinics and labs (score 4.0; effort 3, cost 1, fit 4)
   A per-clinic SaaS that logs every ordered diagnostic test, tracks whether the patient has collected the report, and sends WhatsApp and SMS nudges. It alerts the treating doctor's staff when a report stays uncollected past a set window. The doctor decides what to do; the tool only closes the loop.
   Why now: A recent Express Healthcare piece on an NCDRC negligence ruling says hospitals and labs must coordinate on patient-collected reports, making the open loop a legal and operational gap now.
   First step: Shadow the front desk at one clinic or lab from your internship network for half a day and log every uncollected report and how staff chase it.

4. Scheduled DR drill runner with audit-ready evidence (score 4.0; effort 3, cost 1, fit 4)
   A tool that runs the four DR validation levels (tabletop, planned failover, surprise drill, game day) as scheduled checklists against a small SaaS's infrastructure and produces timestamped evidence reports. Target Indian B2B SaaS teams that need to show recovery readiness to enterprise or regulated customers.
   Why now: AWS's two-part DR series argues recovery targets should be scoped by business decisions and then proven at four escalating levels, a process most small teams skip because it is manual.
   First step: Write a one-page drill template for your own side project covering the four levels, then run a tabletop exercise and record every gap it surfaces.

5. Cache-health and spend guard for LLM app backends (score 4.0; effort 3, cost 1, fit 4)
   A lightweight proxy that sits in front of LLM API calls, tracks prefix-cache hit ratio, token spend per tenant, and throughput, and alerts when cache hit ratio drops below a threshold and stays there. It also flags runaway per-tenant costs. Aim it at Indian startups running agent backends with monthly LLM bills they cannot predict.
   Why now: A new arXiv paper shows prefix-cached agent serving can stay degraded for about 70 minutes after a short burst, and AWS Lambda is reportedly billing idle memory about seven minutes after the last request, so hidden cost drift is now documented.
   First step: Instrument one of your own agent calls with a cache-hit and token-count log for a week, then chart hit ratio against latency to see whether degradation shows up in your traffic.
