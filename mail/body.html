#1 Discharge summary drafter for Indian private hospitals4.2
↻ repeat signal · 4 days
A web tool and API that drafts discharge summaries for long inpatient stays from the EMR notes, labs and medication orders, with every line linked to its source entry. The treating doctor reviews, edits and signs; the tool never adds clinical content of its own. Sold per bed or per discharge to mid-size private hospitals and their HIS vendors.
Effort 3/5 · Cost 2/5 · Fit 5/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: A new npj Digital Medicine study finds AI can help write discharge summaries for long, complex stays, so hospital buyers now have evidence to justify a pilot.
First step: Using your internship contacts, interview two clinicians and one records officer to list the 10 fields their discharge summary template requires and where each one comes from in the EMR.
 
#2 Longitudinal visit-history digest for clinic doctors4.0
↻ repeat signal · 4 days
A tool that turns a patient's past visit notes, prescriptions and lab values into a dated timeline before each follow-up, so the doctor sees what changed and when. It flags missing or conflicting entries for the doctor to resolve. Sold as a monthly plan to single-specialty clinics using a basic EMR or WhatsApp-based record keeping.
Effort 3/5 · Cost 1/5 · Fit 4/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: MedGraph shows that a patient-state graph with dated, per-patient facts is a workable pattern for LLM medical assistants, and it names outdated state and cross-patient leakage as the failure modes to design against.
First step: Take 20 anonymised visit notes from a clinic you know, hand-label the dated medication and diagnosis changes, and check whether a simple per-patient timeline table gets them right before adding any LLM.
 
#3 Vendor credential sweeper for small Indian fintechs4.0
A scanner that inventories a fintech's third-party API keys, OAuth tokens and service accounts, then flags stale or over-scoped ones and tracks rotation with an audit log. Sold to Indian payments, lending and wealth startups that need a DPDP-ready answer to 'who holds our keys'. Priced per vendor integration per month.
Effort 3/5 · Cost 1/5 · Fit 4/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: Chainalysis reported that attackers took credentials and tokens for all its customers, and Gnosis Pay's breach exposed card, identity and wallet data together, so vendor credential exposure is now a board-level question.
First step: Write a checklist of the 15 credential types a typical Spring Boot fintech backend holds, then scan one personal or open-source project against it to test whether detection works.
 
#4 Confidence-based model cascade proxy for LLM apps4.0
↻ repeat signal · 4 days
A drop-in OpenAI-compatible proxy that sends each request to a small model first and escalates to a large model only when a confidence or self-check fails, with per-route cost and quality logs. Sold as a hosted gateway with a self-hosted option to SaaS teams in India running high-volume chat or document pipelines.
Effort 3/5 · Cost 1/5 · Fit 4/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: A Hacker News report says a small model that can call Opus for help matches Opus accuracy at about a quarter of the cost, which makes cascade routing a practical cost lever for teams running agents at volume.
First step: Run the cascade pattern on a 200-item eval set from one of your own GenAI pipelines and measure accuracy and cost against single-model baselines before writing any product code.
 
#5 Omission checker for AI-generated clinical summaries3.8
↻ repeat signal · 2 days
A regression harness that compares AI-generated discharge or visit summaries against their source records and reports dropped medications, dates and allergies for the reviewing doctor. It runs in CI for hospital AI vendors and as an on-demand audit for hospital quality teams. Sold as a per-summary-batch or annual licence.
Effort 3/5 · Cost 2/5 · Fit 4/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: Momentic's QA agents show demand for automated bug-hunting before release, and healthcare leaders at the YourStory roundtable said observability has to be built in from the start, which is a gap for clinical AI vendors.
First step: Build a 30-case fixture set from de-identified or synthetic records with planted omissions, then check whether a simple field-level diff catches all of them before trying an LLM-based judge.
