#3 Durable step log library for long-running agent workflows4.0
↻ repeat signal · 4 days
A small open-source library, in Java or Python, that journals each external tool call and its result to Postgres or SQLite. After a crash, it skips completed steps and rebuilds state from the log. The paid version is a hosted runner for small teams.
Effort 3/5 · Cost 1/5 · Fit 4/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: Martin Fowler's piece on durable execution for multi-day agents and Rivet's S3-backed hibernating actors both point to the same unmet need: agents that survive crashes without a heavy orchestrator.
First step: Build a roughly 100-line Python decorator that journals tool results to SQLite and replays them after a simulated crash, then post it as a Show HN.
 
#4 Closed-loop diagnostic report tracker for clinics and diagnostic labs3.8
A SaaS that tracks every pending diagnostic report, sends reminders to patients over WhatsApp or SMS, and logs when the referring doctor has acknowledged the result. It gives clinics an audit trail that shows the follow-up loop was closed.
Effort 3/5 · Cost 2/5 · Fit 4/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: An NCDRC ruling on medical negligence put the burden of collecting diagnostic reports and coordinating follow-up on hospitals and doctors, so clinics now need evidence that the loop was closed.
First step: Map the full lifecycle of one diagnostic report at a clinic you know, from order to patient collection to doctor acknowledgement, and mark every handoff where a report can be lost.
