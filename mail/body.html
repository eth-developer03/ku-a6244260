#1 Vendor payout reconciliation for Indian marketplaces and agencies4.2
A payouts layer for marketplaces, creator platforms and agencies that pay many vendors each month: validates bank accounts, batches payouts, tracks failures and produces TDS-ready ledgers. Sold as a flat monthly fee per payout volume, with no payment-licence needed because it routes through existing partner banks or gateways.
Effort 3/5 · Cost 2/5 · Fit 5/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: Stripe reports moving $1B of vendor payments to a new payouts system, cutting new-market setup to a week, which makes the pattern concrete for small Indian platforms that still reconcile by spreadsheet.
First step: Interview three Indian marketplace or agency operators about how they pay vendors each month, and map each step from invoice to bank credit to ledger, noting where they spend hours.
 
#2 Scoped, revocable and logged access for AI agents calling SaaS APIs4.0
↻ repeat signal · 2 days
A hosted proxy that sits between a company's AI agents and its SaaS integrations (MCP servers, Google Workspace, payments), enforcing per-agent scopes, a kill switch and an audit log. Sold per agent or per month to Indian SaaS teams shipping agent features who need to show what their agents did.
Effort 3/5 · Cost 1/5 · Fit 4/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: An open-source OAuth2 broker for scoped, revocable agent access has just appeared, and a Fowler piece describes teams hesitating to run agents because they cannot account for production behaviour.
First step: Build a one-week spike that wraps one MCP server behind a proxy that logs every tool call with its scope and lets you revoke an agent's token, then show it to two founders in your network.
 
#3 Discharge summary drafter for small Indian hospitals3.8
↻ repeat signal · 3 days
A per-bed SaaS that turns admission notes, lab results and ward progress entries into a draft discharge summary, which the treating doctor reviews, edits and signs. Sold to 20-100 bed private hospitals and nursing homes that still write these by hand.
Effort 3/5 · Cost 2/5 · Fit 4/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: A new study shows AI can improve discharge summaries for long, complex stays, so hospitals now have evidence that drafting help is worth paying for.
First step: Message three hospital doctors or medical records staff you already know from your internship and ask them to share an anonymised discharge summary template and the top three fields they rewrite most.
 
#4 AI test writer for Indian SaaS teams with small QA headcount3.5
A GitHub app that reads a pull request, generates targeted regression tests for the changed code, runs them in CI and comments only on failures that look real. Priced per repository per month from day one, aimed at 5-30 engineer product companies in India without a dedicated QA team.
Effort 3/5 · Cost 1/5 · Fit 3/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: Momentic's YC launch and Meta's just-in-time testing results show AI-generated tests moving from research into products, but the mid-sized teams that want this are still underserved.
First step: Run a manual version for one friend's startup: take five recent merged PRs, have an LLM draft tests for each, and count how many catch a bug you seeded.
 
#5 On-prem document summariser for clinics that cannot send data to the cloud3.2
A Docker-packaged, self-hosted pipeline that runs a small open model locally to OCR and summarise scanned lab reports and referral letters, with no patient data leaving the premises. Sold as a one-time licence plus support to clinics and diagnostic centres with strict data-residency rules.
Effort 4/5 · Cost 3/5 · Fit 4/5 (lower effort and cost are better)
Why now: YC is now actively calling for on-prem frontier AI for hospitals and banks because those buyers cannot use hosted APIs, which opens a gap for a small vendor serving clinics below enterprise scale.
First step: Benchmark a quantised open OCR and summarisation model on 30 anonymised report scans you already have from your internship, and record accuracy and hardware needs.
